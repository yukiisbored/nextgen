Ever since I've had a partner, I have been playing a lot of co-op games recently because we're in a long distance relationship and it's best way for us to bond despite our physical distance.
Recently, a friend of mine started a similar relationship too and we kept talking about what games to play with our partners.

So, I thought, I should make a list of coop games that I have found and the various quirks with them.
From experience, this is really not something that's well documented online nor on store fronts.

Even markers like "Online Co-op" on Steam are not very helpful because they don't really tell you much other than "it exists officially in some form".
That doesn't tell you the amount of mods that exist for the game, whether it's cross-platform or not, and so on.

I'll be updating this list regularly as I discover more games and of course, you can contribute to this list by sending me an e-mail or submitting a pull request/issue on GitHub: https://github.com/yukiisbored/nextgen.

## Playing split-screen games remotely

Split-screen games can be played remotely via streaming. This can be done with [Steam Remote Play] (if supported by the game) or [Parsec].

[Steam Remote Play]: https://store.steampowered.com/remoteplay
[Parsec]: https://parsec.app/

## Playing with servers without port-forwarding

Some games require a dedicated server to play together and you're unable to setup port-forwarding or spend money on a server.
You can use services like [Playit.gg] to tunnel your local server to the internet for free.
Of course, you can also setup a VPN with [Hamachi] or [Tailscale].

[Playit.gg]: https://playit.gg/
[Hamachi]: https://vpn.net/
[Tailscale]: https://tailscale.com/

## Emulating old games

### Split-screen games on consoles

A lot of games that exists before the seventh generation of consoles (PS3/Xbox 360/Wii) have split-screen multiplayer and can be emulated on PC without hefty system requirements.

A friend specifically recommended PlayStation 2 games, such as Kuri Kuri Mix (US: Cookies & Cream), Champions of Norrath, Baldur's Gate Dark Alliance (1+2), and Gauntlet Dark Legacy.
I think these games can be emulated with [PCSX2] and played with a friend remotely via [Parsec].

I have made a query on MobyGames of [PlayStation 2 games with split-screen multiplayer].

[PCSX2]: https://pcsx2.net/
[PlayStation 2 games with split-screen multiplayer]: https://www.mobygames.com/game/attribute:156/include_dlc:false/include_nsfw:false/platform:ps2/release_status:all/sort:title/page:1/

### DOSBOX

A lot of old PC games from the DOS era have local LAN multiplayer which can be setup easily with IPX/emulation in DOSBOX.

I have made a query on MobyGames of [games with Multiplayer via IPX support].
